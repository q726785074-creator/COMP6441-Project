# PhishWise participant testing plan

## Purpose

The study will examine whether the activity helps beginner university students recognise phishing evidence and choose safer responses. Functional website testing is separate from this participant evaluation.

## Participants

- Intended group: university students aged 18 or over with little or no formal cybersecurity education
- Planned number: [enter after approval]
- Recruitment method: [enter method]
- Exclusion rule: cybersecurity students or professionals may review accuracy, but their results will not be mixed with the beginner sample

Participation must be voluntary. Before starting, explain the educational purpose, the local data handling, the expected time, the right to stop, and the instruction not to enter personal information.

## Procedure

1. Record an anonymous session code, activity version, interface language, and consent status.
2. Ask the participant to complete the formal flow without Demo Mode.
3. Preserve the first submitted pre-test score, first scenario completions, first defence choices, and post-test score.
4. Ask the participant to complete the anonymous feedback form.
5. Note navigation or wording problems without recording identity details.
6. Export or transcribe only the consented anonymous measures listed below.

## Evidence to collect

- Pre-test total out of 10
- Post-test total out of 10
- Change in score
- Topic-level pre-test and post-test outcomes
- First-attempt scores for the three inspection scenarios
- First-attempt defence score out of 7
- Clarity, interaction helpfulness, and difficulty ratings
- Confidence and recommendation responses
- De-identified optional comments, after removing accidental personal information

## Planned analysis

- Report the number of valid participants and incomplete sessions.
- Compare each participant's pre-test and post-test scores without treating the tests as identical questions.
- Summarise topic errors, scenario performance, and defence choices.
- Report rating counts or medians and group de-identified comments by theme.
- Discuss small sample size, convenience sampling, different question difficulty, immediate testing, and self-reported confidence as limitations.
- Revise unclear material only after documenting the original version and the evidence supporting the change.

## Safety and data handling

Use fictional examples only. Do not ask for names, email addresses, student numbers, passwords, or real incident details. Keep anonymous results separate from free-text facilitator notes. Store the minimum evidence required by the course and delete it according to the approved course process. Demo Mode values must never be included as participant results.
